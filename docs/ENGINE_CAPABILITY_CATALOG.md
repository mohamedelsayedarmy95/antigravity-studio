# Engine Capability Catalog

The schema files define the exact fields and defaults. The examples are reusable configurations from the current engine. Render examples exercise representative combinations and make no guarantees for every permutation. Historical M1, M2, and Fieldnote evidence is indexed in [Session handoff](SESSION_HANDOFF.md).

| Capability and implementation | Configuration and reusable example | Verification and status | Performance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3D stage and scene graph — [Stage](../src/stage3d/Stage.tsx), [plan](../src/stage3d/plan.ts), [nodes](../src/stage3d/nodes), [schema](../src/stage3d/schema.ts) | Primitive shapes, parametric morphs, extruded local SVGs, particles and void, gradient or grid backgrounds. Reuse the [M1](../src/films/m1-core-test.ts) morph and particle scene or the brand SVG reveal. | **Implemented and tested.** M1 renders and compiler tests cover representative nodes, scene timing, duplicate IDs and references. Schema maxima are input limits rather than render budgets. | Half-float render targets, dense morph grids and particles use GPU time and memory. Tune resolution and counts on the delivery host. |
| Camera rigs — [camera solver](../src/stage3d/camera/rig.ts) and stage schema | Lens and target tracks, explicit distance or radius/fill, orbit, push/pull, crane, truck, handheld and beat impulse. Reuse M1's push, orbit and downbeat combination. | **Implemented and tested** for representative configurations through M1. Compare adjacent frames to evaluate pace and framing. | Frame-driven math. Camera changes can increase on-screen overdraw or alter focus cost. Per-rig timings are unverified. |
| Lighting rigs — [rigs](../src/stage3d/lighting/rigs.ts) | `stripRim`, `studioSoftbox` and `neonGels`, with intensity and environment rotation tracks. Reuse the animated strip rim on M1 chrome. | **Implemented and tested** through M1 and M2 fixtures. Physical plausibility remains a shot review. | PMREM generation costs startup time and GPU memory. Rotation animates the existing environment. |
| Materials and shaders — [library](../src/stage3d/materials/library.ts), [GLSL](../src/stage3d/shaders/glsl.ts) | Chrome, liquid chrome, brushed metal, glass, iridescent glass, ceramic, obsidian, emissive and wire presets. Configure colors and material property tracks. Reuse the [Obsidian swept sheet](../src/engine/client/studio-obsidian.ts). | **Implemented and tested** for representative morph, glass, particle and Obsidian scenes. The suite does not isolate every preset. | Transmission and additional depth work can increase render cost. Geometry density and particle coverage matter more than the number of configured names. |
| Post-processing — [ScenePipeline](../src/stage3d/post/ScenePipeline.ts), [compositor](../src/stage3d/transitions/Compositor.ts) | ACES/AgX, exposure, optional bloom, target or fixed DOF, chromatic aberration, vignette and seeded grain. Film overrides can be refined per scene. Reuse M1's target-focus DOF. | **Implemented and tested** in representative M1/M2 and Studio combinations. [Finishing tests](../tests/studio-finishing.test.ts) cover composition of finishing options. | DOF runs at half resolution and bloom uses a mip chain. Transmissive geometry with particles can need an extra depth pass. |
| Motion and springs — [primitives](../src/motion/MotionPrimitives.tsx), [spring presets](../src/motion/springPresets.ts), [tracks](../src/engine/time/animatable.ts) | Frame and FPS driven spring presets, masked/glyph entrances, slide blur, levitation and swipe. Use `appleHero` with the core text layer and use FilmSpec tracks for spatial transforms. | **Implemented and tested** for tracks, beat impulses and typography reveal through engine tests and M2. The standalone legacy primitives are **implemented but unverified** by the current production suite. | Heavy blur, many DOM elements and per-glyph effects can increase browser capture time. Legacy primitives still reference the legacy font helper. |
| Scene transitions — [plan](../src/stage3d/plan.ts), [compositor](../src/stage3d/transitions/Compositor.ts) | Cut, dissolve, noise dissolve, light sweep, glitch slice and zoom through with a duration and color. Reuse M2's `lightSweep` at `1b`. | **Implemented and tested** for cut/dissolve timing and representative M1/M2 effects. [Boundary tests](../tests/stage-boundaries.test.ts) cover frame alignment. | A transition can render two scenes into separate targets. Prefer a cut when it reads better or when overlapping the heaviest scenes exceeds budget. |
| 2D overlays and device presentation — [Film](../src/engine/Film.tsx), [ScreenLayer](../src/layer2d/ScreenLayer.tsx), [screen geometry](../src/engine/client/screen.ts), [overlay schema](../src/layer2d/schema.ts) | Text, counters, charts and HUD. Screenshot and recording layers support contain/cover, crop, focal point, trim and per-aspect framing. Reuse the [Factory render fixture](../tests/factory-render.integration.ts) or use Studio Cut for a branded screen treatment. | **Implemented and tested.** Screen tests cover geometry and media frame windows. Render integration covers real assets generated for the test and independent still captures. | Recording preparation writes decoded frame sequences and uses scratch space. Rendering the UI in the DOM keeps it independent of 3D post-processing. |
| Typography and RTL — [TextView](../src/layer2d/text/TextView.tsx), [layout](../src/layer2d/layout.ts), [local fonts](../src/typography/localFonts.ts) | Role-driven type, anchors, fit, reveal modes and bundled Latin Inter, JetBrains Mono and Archivo. Use an M2 text overlay for Latin titles. For Arabic runs, the renderer keeps text together and lets the browser set direction. | Latin registry and overlay **implemented and tested**. Arabic joining fallback is **implemented but unverified** across target machines and mixed-direction layouts. A bundled shaping-safe RTL font and per-locale visual qualification are **planned**. | Bundled font readiness gates rendering. System fallback can change glyphs and metrics across machines. |
| Audio analysis and sync — [beat grid](../src/engine/time/beatGrid.ts), [reactive hooks](../src/audio/useAudioReactive.ts), [audio assembly](../scripts/lib/editorial-audio.ts) | Manual BPM/grid, authored cues and gain, FFmpeg measurement, 48 kHz stereo AAC. Reuse the [audio test](../tests/editorial-audio.test.ts) cue offsets. A separate local FFT hook estimates bass, mid and treble energy. | Manual grid, cues and measured audio assembly **implemented and tested**. FFT-driven visuals **implemented but unverified** in current factory delivery. Inferred downbeat detection and narration workflows are **planned** capabilities. | FFT analysis and whole-stream decode/EBU measurement cost processing time. Media decode and mixing stay local. |
| Editorial shot recipes — [Studio Cut](../src/engine/client/studio-cut.ts), [shot recipes](../src/engine/client/shot-recipes.ts), [focus](../src/engine/client/focus.ts) | Versioned Studio Cut v1/v2, expressive motion, authored focus/taps/results, per-aspect safe areas and opt-in Obsidian. Reuse the recipe for a guided proof shot whose result is tied to the recording frame. | **Implemented and tested** for recipe timing, UI registration, observed results and representative Fieldnote masters. Obsidian remains **experimental** as an art direction despite its geometry and timing tests. | Extra geometry and layered 2D effects add render work. Validate the crops and readability in each target aspect. |
| Rendering and delivery — [Factory](../scripts/factory.ts), [renderer](../scripts/lib/client-render.ts), [delivery](../scripts/lib/delivery.ts) | Preview stills/ranges/full MP4, 30 fps SDR master with optional editorial mix, local review encode, and evidence-gated final local handoff. Reuse the [acceptance commands](CREATIVE_ACCEPTANCE_GATES.md). | **Implemented and tested** for current format contracts, with historical three-ratio render evidence and current focused governance tests. Platform-specific approval, HDR and other master codecs are **planned**. | Masters verify a complete decode and render two PNG samples per aspect. Review encode adds one transcode. Final handoff copies the verified master. |

Measured production budgets vary with aspect, machine, scene complexity, and browser backend. The historical handoff indexes prior render and memory measurements. Re-measure on the intended deployment hardware when the job needs a performance guarantee.
